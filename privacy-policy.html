Privacy Policy

Bolt & Craft Studio ("the App") is developed and published by MaraQueens Corp ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not require users to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to play Bolt & Craft Studio.

The App saves your gameplay progress, unlocked blueprints, coins, and settings locally on your device using local storage.

2. Advertising and Analytics

To support free gameplay, Bolt & Craft Studio integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Advertising ID or IDFA) to serve relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

For more details on Google's privacy practices, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Bolt & Craft Studio is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to remove it.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us device information. We employ commercially reasonable measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted.
